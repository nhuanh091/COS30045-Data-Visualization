TV Energy Consumption Data Visualizations

Exploring energy efficiency in Australian televisions through various chart types.

Scatter Plot: Energy Consumption vs Star Rating

This scatter plot shows the relationship between energy consumption (kWh/year) and star rating for various TV models. Higher star ratings indicate better energy efficiency.

Donut Chart: Energy by Screen Technology (All Sizes)

Average energy consumption across all TV sizes, grouped by screen technology (LCD, LED, OLED). OLED TVs tend to consume more energy due to their premium display technology.

Bar Chart: Energy by Screen Technology (55-inch TVs)

Average energy consumption for 55-inch TVs only, comparing different screen technologies. This focuses on a specific screen size to control for size variations.

Line Chart: Spot Power Prices (1998-2024)

Historical spot electricity prices across Australian states from 1998 to 2024. Shows trends in energy costs that influence appliance efficiency decisions.
